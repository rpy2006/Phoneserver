6. Disclaimers

  • As-is software. The application and documentation are provided without warranty, per the Terms of Service.
  • Local-network scope. The product is designed, documented and supported for a trusted LAN. Public-internet deployment, port forwarding and reverse proxies are outside that scope and unsupported.
  • Security posture. Known limitations — plaintext credential storage, unauthenticated endpoints when no password is set, absence of TLS, fixed ports 8080/8090, and heuristic ad-fraud checks — are disclosed in the security model. Their disclosure is not an invitation to rely on the product beyond that scope.

The publisher does not operate it, does not receive its data, and is not a party to campaigns, creatives, billing or payouts you configure. You are responsible for advertising content and legal compliance.
  • Data. The publisher does not operate a backend and receives none of your content, credentials, ad-network records or usage data — see the privacy policy.
